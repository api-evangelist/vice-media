In April 1987, Sean Penn was arrested for punching an extra on the set of the movie Colors. The actor ended up serving 27 days in the Men’s Central Jail in Los Angeles, during which time he encountered another well-known inmate: serial killer Richard Ramirez (a.k.a. the Night Stalker).
